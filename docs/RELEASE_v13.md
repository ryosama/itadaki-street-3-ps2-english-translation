# English v13

Partial English fan translation of Itadaki Street 3 for PS2, Japanese **SLPM-62112**.
Owner-tested successfully with **PCSX2 v2.8.2**.

v13 adds 119 string replacements covering all twelve standard Free Play opponent
profiles, statistics, empty player/trainee slots and the controller-identification
prompt. Previous Rules, Chance, Tutor/menu/map translations and both English title
images are preserved. Independent full BPS reconstruction and checksum tests passed.

## Apply

Use **Floating IPS (Flips)**: https://github.com/Sir-Walrus/Flips

Extract this ZIP, choose Apply Patch, select `Itadaki_Street_3_English_v13.bps`,
then your **original, unpatched SLPM-62112 ISO**. Save to a new ISO. Do not overwrite
the original or apply this cumulative patch to v12. Boot the new ISO rather than
restoring an older savestate.

## Limits

This is **not a complete translation**. Most Tutor pages, many dialogues and shop
names, and some Training/Quest/UI text remain Japanese. The red opponent-selection
participation badge and card artwork/credits also remain Japanese. Some profile
labels and names are abbreviated to fit; `W/R` means win ratio and `Like` means hobby.
Already-stored names in memory-card saves may remain Japanese. Physical PS2 hardware
has not been tested; owner testing is not exhaustive.

No ISO, BIOS, emulator, private saves or custom Apply launcher is included.
Use your own original game image. Report problems with version, mode, map and
screenshots, not game/BIOS/save binaries.
